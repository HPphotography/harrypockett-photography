Observations on Early Light

Wandering quiet city streets before dawn and noting directional shadows

The Pockett Journal — Note 01

The Solitude of Morning

There is an undisturbed stillness right before dawn breaks. Long shadows stretch across empty avenues, offering clean geometries that disappear as soon as the day begins.

Learning to wait for moments to compose themselves naturally rather than forcing an artificial perspective upon the scene.

Journal Entry — 02

The Value of Analog Practice

Slowing down rhythm through film formats and deliberate framing

▼
The Pockett Journal — Note 02

Frame Discipline & Tactile Grain

Carrying a camera with 36 exposures changes how you evaluate light. Every click requires consideration, sharpening anticipation and focus. Not seeing the image instantly allows you to evaluate your work with fresh eyes days later during development.

Notes on maintaining visual tone across varied projects

The Pockett Journal — Note 03

Finding the Thread

Style isn't a preset filter; it is a consistent way of seeing space, geometry, and human connection across different contexts. Removing good photos that don't serve the broader narrative is often harder than capturing them in the first place.
