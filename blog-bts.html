Lightweight Rigging on Location

When working in dynamic environments, heavy studio strobes often limit spontaneity. Here, we opted for compact battery-powered heads with modified grids to maintain clean highlights while moving fast.

Interfacing subtle continuous light with late afternoon ambient shadows allows the subject to feel grounded naturally in the space without looking overly artificial.
